OBJECTIVE
=========
Solve one Rush Hour puzzle by sliding its arrow block(s) out of the 6x6 grid.
Entering a puzzle is only preparation, NOT completion. If a puzzle is already
visible, continue playing it rather than reopening menus or restarting.

The playfield is on the left of the image. The board number and move counter
are on the right; they are not part of the 6x6 grid.

CONTROLS: CHIP-8 HEXADECIMAL KEYS
================================
 Hex key   PC key   Use
 ----------------------
 5         W        up
 8         S        down
 7         A        left
 9         D        right
 A         Z        confirm / hold while sliding a block
 1         1        in-game options / back

Use the HEX key values, not the PC labels. Hexadecimal A is confirm/slide;
hexadecimal 7 is left. They are different keys.

PLAYING A PUZZLE
================
Direction keys WITHOUT A move the round selection cursor across the grid.
To slide the block under the cursor, hold A AND the desired direction in
THE SAME action:
  [A, 5] = slide up;    [A, 8] = slide down;
  [A, 7] = slide left;  [A, 9] = slide right.
Blocks slide along their own orientation and cannot pass through other blocks.
A by itself does not slide a block. Do not press opposite directions together.
Do not use key 1 while a playable board is visible unless you deliberately
need the options menu; it is not a movement key.

Before choosing a move, identify the arrow block(s), their exit direction,
and the blocks obstructing their path. Work backward from the exit: move
obstructing blocks into available spaces so the arrow block can leave.
Navigate the cursor to the block you intend to move, then use an A+direction
chord to slide it. Moving a non-arrow block to clear space can be necessary.

Start with 8-frame actions for cursor navigation and block slides, then inspect
the result. Use a longer allowed duration if the input did not register. Compare
the cursor, block positions, and move counter with the previous image to tell
cursor navigation from an actual block move. If a slide is blocked, choose a
different direction or reposition another block instead of repeating it.

A static puzzle is waiting for YOUR INPUT. No-key actions will not solve it.
When several moves seem plausible, choose a purposeful legal move and inspect
the result rather than waiting indefinitely. Reconsider unsuccessful moves
using the recent display/action history.

MENUS AND TIMING
================
The emulator is paused while you decide. Each action frame advances only
1/60 second, regardless of how much real time passes. The controller releases
all keys after each action and normally advances additional released-key
settling frames before presenting the next image. You do not need a separate
no-key action merely to release a key.

If still at the Play menu, press A for 60 frames. If at board-set selection,
confirm the initial board set with A for 60 frames. Once the puzzle appears,
switch to playing it; do not keep pressing A alone as if still in a menu.
Wait with no keys only when startup, a transition, or an animation actually
needs more emulated time. Do not wait simply because a finished board image
is unchanged.

COMPLETION
==========
Do not assess the goal as complete merely because a menu was confirmed, a
puzzle appeared, the cursor moved, or the move counter increased. Require clear
visual evidence that the arrow block(s) escaped and the board was solved,
such as a victory indication or advancement to the next board after an exit.
